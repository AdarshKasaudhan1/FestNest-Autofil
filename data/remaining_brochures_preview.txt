context behind the book.
▪ The essence of the book - distil the most important 
ideas, arguments and insights.
▪ Critical perspective - go beyond summary. examine 
ideas, perspectives, strengths and limitations.
▪ Real-world connections - connect the book's insights 
with corporate situations, leadership, management 
and everyday life.
▪ Your take - share your team's interpretation, 
reflections and key learnings.

▪ Maximum: 8 minutes
